GestureTune

Music. Without the Touch.

An AI-powered gesture-controlled music application designed to make music interaction more natural, accessible and hands-free. Control your music with simple hand gestures while enjoying personalized favourites and playlists.

The Problem

Why GestureTune?

Traditional music applications depend heavily on touch controls. This can be inconvenient when a user's hands are occupied, wet, dirty, during workouts, or when the phone is placed at a distance. GestureTune explores a natural, touch-free alternative using computer vision.

👋

A Natural User Interface

Instead of searching for buttons on a screen, users can perform predefined hand gestures to control common music actions.

Project Objective

  • Reduce dependence on touch-based music controls.
  • Provide intuitive real-time gesture recognition.
  • Offer personalized accounts and favourite songs.
  • Create a smooth and accessible music-listening experience.

How It Works

From gesture to music action.

02 · DetectionHand landmarks are detected.
03 · RecognitionThe gesture is classified.
04 · MappingGesture is mapped to a music command.
